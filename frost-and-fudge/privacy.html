Frost & Fudge – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

Frost & Fudge is a home bakery in Madambakkam, Chennai, run by Stella. The Frost & Fudge Android app (package com.rajlabs.frostandfudge, published by RajLabs) lets you browse our cakes, cupcakes, cookies and brownies and place orders. This policy explains what we collect and why, in plain language.

Contact: arajkumar1784@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

  • When you place an order: your name, phone number, the items you order, your cake choices (flavour, shape, tiers, add-ons, colour, theme, essence, cake message) and any notes you add, plus your preferred date and time slot and whether you choose pickup or delivery.
  • If you choose delivery: your delivery address, landmark and pincode, used only to check that we deliver to your area and to deliver your order.
  • Reference photos: if you choose to attach a cake design photo while ordering, it is stored with your order only so we can make your cake. It is visible only to Frost & Fudge admins, never shared, and deleted on request.
  • Payment amounts: the order total, advance and balance amounts, and whether they have been paid, so we can keep track of your order.
  • When you apply through “Bake with us”: your name, phone number, area, what you bake, and any photos you choose to upload.
  • Bakery owners (admins only): email address used to sign in.
  • On your device only: the app remembers your name, phone and address to pre-fill your next order, and caches the menu, announcements and delivery areas. This stays on your phone.

We do not collect your GPS location, contacts, payment details, or use advertising or analytics tracking. The app shows no ads.

2. Why we use it

Only to receive, prepare and deliver your order, contact you about it, and to review baker applications. We do not use your data for marketing profiles.

3. Payments

You pay us directly via UPI outside the app. For cakes we may ask for an advance (for example 50%) with the balance paid at pickup or delivery. The app never sees or stores your UPI, bank or card details.

4. Who processes your data

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), provided by Google LLC, stores order and application data and handles admin sign-in on our behalf as a service provider. See Firebase privacy.
  • WhatsApp: when you place an order, the app opens WhatsApp with your order details pre-filled so you can send it to us. That message is handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under WhatsApp’s privacy policy.
  • Delivery: if your order is sent by a courier or delivery partner, we share only your name, phone number and address with them to deliver it.

5. We never sell your data

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone for advertising or any other purpose.

6. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to orders is limited to the bakery owners, who sign in with a password.

7. How long we keep it

Order records (including addresses and reference photos) are kept for up to 12 months for our bookkeeping and repeat-order help, then deleted. Baker applications are deleted within 6 months if we don’t work together. You can ask us to delete earlier at any time.

8. Your choices & deletion

To see, correct or delete your data, email arajkumar1784@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp with your name and phone number. We will act within 30 days. You can clear data saved on your phone by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it.

9. Children

The app is meant for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If we update this policy we will change the effective date above and post it at this same address.
